A few things to know before you test

Candar is built around user choice and control. These are a few features worth understanding before you start testing.

Map & Location Safety

You control whether you appear on the Candar Map. Map presence stops when Candar is backgrounded, the phone is locked, you log out, disable Show on Map, use Ghost Mode or hide your profile, or leave Candar inactive for about three minutes. Candar also uses a server-side safety expiry so stale locations do not remain visible. Your green online indicator is separate from Map sharing.

Try it: Appear on the Map, leave Candar untouched for a few minutes and watch your Map presence disappear. Use Candar again and see it return.

Ghost Mode & Privacy

Candar includes controls for profile visibility and Map visibility so you decide when and how other members can discover you.

Try it: Switch privacy settings on and off and check that your visibility changes as expected.

Likes & Favourites

Likes show another member that you are interested. Favourites are a separate way to keep track of profiles you want to find again.

Try it: Like and Favourite profiles from different screens and check that the state stays consistent.

Messages & Shared Photos

Candar messaging includes reactions, photo sharing and shared albums as part of the conversation experience.

Try it: Send messages, react to them and open shared photos or albums from both sides of a chat.

View on Map

When a member is actively sharing their Map presence, their profile can offer View on Map. When that Map presence ends, the option should no longer be available.

Try it: Watch a member become active and inactive on the Map and check that View on Map follows.
